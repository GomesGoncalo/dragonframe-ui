How suggestions work

Each group keeps one photo, outlined and marked Keeping. It starts as the suggestion: the sharpest, best exposed shot. The frame also looks at faces, slowly, taking the groups on your screen first; once a group has been looked at, its suggestion favours open eyes and smiles, with sharpness as the tie-breaker. Tap a photo to see it large and compare, and choose "Keep this one" to change the choice. Nothing is deleted until you press a Delete button and confirm. Groups are by appearance, not identical files, and the suggestions come from small automatic models that can be wrong.

Sync a Google Photos album to the frame

One shared-album link per line (in Google Photos: Share, then Create link). The frame downloads the photos itself, and never downloads a photo it already has.


      

    

  


  

    

      
Calendars the frame follows

      
One calendar link per line. For a public Google calendar, its ordinary sharing or embedding link will do. For a private one, use its "Secret address in iCal format" (in Google Calendar: Settings, then the calendar). Any address that ends in .ics or starts with webcal:// works too; in Apple Calendar, share the calendar as a public calendar and copy its link. The frame fetches each calendar itself every 15 minutes. It only reads them: nothing is ever written back.

Night

      
The frame switches its screen off for the night and back on in the morning. It can also go dark whenever the room does. A frame that was touched in the last five minutes stays on.

      
      
      
      

    

    

      
Sound

      
      
Events with a time are announced with a soft chime and a note on the screen. Nothing sounds while the screen is off for the night.
