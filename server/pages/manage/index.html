How suggestions work

Each group keeps one photo, outlined and marked Keeping. It starts as the suggestion: the sharpest, best exposed shot. The frame also looks at faces, slowly, taking the groups on your screen first; once a group has been looked at, its suggestion favours open eyes and smiles, with sharpness as the tie-breaker. Tap a photo to see it large and compare, and choose "Keep this one" to change the choice. Nothing is deleted until you press a Delete button and confirm. Groups are by appearance, not identical files, and the suggestions come from small automatic models that can be wrong.

Sync a Google Photos album to the frame

One shared-album link per line (in Google Photos: Share, then Create link). The frame downloads the photos itself, and never downloads a photo it already has.


      

    

  


  

    

      
Calendars the frame follows

      
One calendar link per line. For a public Google calendar, its ordinary sharing or embedding link will do. For a private one, use its "Secret address in iCal format" (in Google Calendar: Settings, then the calendar). Any address that ends in .ics or starts with webcal:// works too; in Apple Calendar, share the calendar as a public calendar and copy its link. The frame fetches each calendar itself every 15 minutes. It only reads them: nothing is ever written back.

Reminders

      
A reminder is a note the frame puts on its screen ahead of an event and keeps there until someone touches it. Each rule applies to every event, on any calendar, whose name has one of the rule's words: "bin, recycling" finds "Black bin", "Bins out" and "Recycling week" but not "Robin". Put the events on a calendar as usual (for the bins, an event that repeats every week or fortnight) and the rule does the rest. A rule can also have the frame do something when its note comes up, such as play a radio station.

      

        
        
        
        
        
        
        
      

      

    

    

      
Getting there

      
For events you travel to, the frame works out when to leave and says so on its screen from half an hour before. Say where home is, then set up each kind of event once: where it is and how you get there. Every later event with the same name and location is reached the same way. Postcodes are the surest thing to type; addresses and station names usually work.

Tasks

      
A task is somebody's, or anyone's (whoever ticks it on the frame says who they are). It is done once, on a day or whenever, or comes round on the days of the week you choose. Ticking it on the frame gives its points. A task done once leaves the list the day after it is done.

Tasks from the calendar

      
Calendar events can be tasks. A rule applies to every event, on any calendar, whose name has one of its words ("swimming, football" finds "Swimming lesson"): on the day of the event it is in that person's list on the frame, to be ticked like any task, and is worth the rule's points.

Radio stations

      
The frame plays these itself, from each station's own address: on its screen under Utilities, Music; from the remote control above; or by a reminder rule (Calendar), which makes a station that starts with an event an alarm clock. Find a station by its name, in the directory at radio-browser.info (which is sent only what is typed here).

Spotify

      

      
      
With this on, the frame appears as a speaker called "Frame" in the Spotify app of anyone on this Wi-Fi (the devices button, beside the play button), as a smart speaker does. It needs Spotify Premium. Nobody signs in on the frame and nothing of anyone's account is kept there: the app hands over what is needed when the frame is chosen, and after the frame restarts it has to be chosen again. Starting a radio station hands the music back to the app.

    

    

      
House

      

      
The frame can show the doorbell, cameras, alarm and door sensors that Home Assistant knows about. It does not sign in to them: a small relay beside Home Assistant tells the frame what to show, and proves itself with a key made here. See the README for setting the relay up.

      

      

        
        
      

    

    

      
Night

      
The frame switches its screen off for the night and back on in the morning. It can also go dark whenever the room does. A frame that was touched in the last five minutes stays on.

      
      
      
      

    

    

      
Sound

      
      
Events with a time are announced with a soft chime and a note on the screen. Nothing sounds while the screen is off for the night.
